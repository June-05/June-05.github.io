  1. Where It Started

    My journey into IT began in 12th grade when I encountered a real-world logistical problem. An unprecedented influx of enrollees in my academic strand led to highly unbalanced classroom sizes. Instead of just seeing a chaotic situation, I saw an opportunity to build a systemic fix.

  2. The First Solution

    I developed an automated Google Sheets system to evenly distribute students and pitched the solution to my adviser. This initiative evolved into my very first technical project: the "Student Distribution System."

    ​Building this tool was a major turning point.It made me realize my passion for using technology to solve real-world problems.

  3. Looking Ahead

    Moving forward, my goal is to integrate the latest technologies to build intuitive, user-centered solutions—creating digital applications that people genuinely want to use every day.
